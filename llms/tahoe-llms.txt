# Tahoe

> Tahoe (Tahoe Therapeutics) is an AI-biotech company building "the ground truth for biological superintelligence" — gigascale single-cell datasets and perturbation-trained foundation models of the human cell, used to design therapeutics starting with cancer. Tahoe publishes no commercial REST API; its programmatic surface is open-source models, datasets, and Python tooling.

## Products
- Tahoe-100M: the largest interventional single-cell dataset to date — 100M+ transcriptomes capturing responses to ~1,100 chemical perturbations across 50 cancer cell lines.
- Tahoe-x1 (Tx1): a family of perturbation-trained single-cell foundation models with up to 3 billion parameters; fully open-source with open weights, training and evaluation code.
- Mosaic platform: scalable in vivo single-cell data generation mapping how drugs affect patient-derived cells.
